# Product vision

Guitar Studio will help acoustic and electric guitarists build personal song arrangements and practice deliberately: song lyrics with provenance, authored chords and structured tablature, an interactive fretboard and focused practice sessions.

The player remains in control. Later microphone tools suggest pitches and possible positions locally; the player confirms before notes enter an arrangement. Lyrics may be manually entered or imported only with appropriate rights, and a song can exist without lyrics.

Sprint 0 establishes the code, validation, infrastructure and architectural boundaries. Song CRUD, editor behavior, tuner, recording, lyrics providers, authentication and final visual design are future work. Sprint 1 should agree a narrow editable song/arrangement slice and acceptance examples before broadening features.
